Multi-turn image editing study

Thank you for helping! This takes about 10 minutes. Please use a computer rather than a phone.

On each screen you will see an original image and two results, A and B. Both were made by applying the same 10 edit instructions one after another to the original. Use the slider (or the Previous edit / Next edit buttons next to it) to step through the edits; the instruction of the edit you are looking at is shown above the slider.

For each comparison, answer three questions:

  1. Image quality – looking at A and B on their own, which looks more natural, throughout the edits and especially at the last edit? Look for:
    • patchy areas
    • unnatural-looking parts
    • artifacts
    • blur
  2. Instruction following – which result better carries out the edit instructions? Did each edit take effect, on the right object? An edit can be done in more than one way; any reasonable way counts.
  3. Preservation – compared with the original, which result better keeps the parts that no instruction asked to change (people, background, other objects)?

A result can follow every instruction and still look patchy or unnatural; that counts against it on image quality.

If A and B are about equal on a question, choose About the same. There are no right answers; go with your impression. You can go back to earlier comparisons with the Previous button.

Your screen is narrow. The images will be small; a computer is recommended.
